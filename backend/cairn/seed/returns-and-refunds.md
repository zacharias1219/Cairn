# Returns and refunds policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must be unused, with tags attached, in their original packaging.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once a refund is issued, it usually takes 5-10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank.

## Items we cannot refund
Gift cards, personalised items and final-sale clearance items cannot be refunded. Worn footwear can be exchanged for a size issue within 30 days but not refunded.

## Damaged or faulty items
If an item arrives damaged or develops a fault, contact us within 30 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund, and we cover return shipping.
