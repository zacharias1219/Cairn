# Shipping policy

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the contiguous United States. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days. Orders placed before 2pm Mountain Time ship the same business day.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on orders over $75. Below $75, standard shipping costs $6.95 and express shipping costs $14.95.

## Tracking
You will receive a tracking number by email when your order ships. Tracking usually starts updating within 24 hours.

## Changing a shipping address
We can change the shipping address while an order is still processing. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed, but you can ask the carrier to redirect the package.
