# Exchanges and gift cards

## Exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different size or colour within 30 days of delivery. We ship the replacement as soon as the original is scanned by the carrier, and exchange shipping is free.

## Gift cards
Acme gift cards never expire and can be used online or in any Acme store. Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash or refunded.

## Price adjustments
If an item you bought drops in price within 14 days of purchase, we will refund the difference once on request.
