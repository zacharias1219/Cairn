# Warranty and repairs

## Lifetime repair promise
Acme packs and tents carry a lifetime warranty: they are covered against defects in materials and workmanship for the life of the product. If something fails because of a defect, we will repair it, replace it, or issue store credit.

## Normal wear and accidents
Normal wear and tear, accidents and misuse are not covered by the warranty, but our repair shop can fix most damage for a fee. Repair quotes are free.

## Making a claim
Start a warranty claim by sharing your order number and a photo of the issue. Most claims are decided within 5 business days.
