# International orders and privacy

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union. International delivery takes 7-14 business days. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout so there are no surprise fees on delivery.

## Your data
You can ask us to export or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. We complete deletion requests within 72 hours and send a receipt when it is done.

## Contact hours
Our support team is available every day from 7am to 7pm Mountain Time. The assistant is available around the clock.
