# Trail Club membership

## What members get
Trail Club costs $9 a month and includes free express shipping, early access to new gear, and a 10% member price on full-price items. Trail Club Plus costs $15 a month and adds one free gear repair each year.

## Cancelling
You can cancel Trail Club at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep member benefits until then. We do not refund partial months.

## Pausing
Members can pause Trail Club for up to 3 months once a year instead of cancelling.
